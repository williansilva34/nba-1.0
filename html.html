The best Site of NBA

The basketball is one of the basic pieces of equipment for playing basketball.
Its main manufacturers include Molten, Adidas, Nike, Spalding and Wilson. The first basketball was manufactured in 1891 by the American company A.C. Spading & Brothers.

History.

The NBA began in 1946 with 11 teams and, through a series of expansions, reductions, and team relocations, currently consists of 30 teams.
The United States is home to 29 teams; another is in Canada.
The current organization of the league divides the 30 teams into two conferences of three divisions with five teams each.
